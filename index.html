Choices that matter

26 endings so far, plus battle feats, all collected in a stamp book. Stuck on one? Trade treats for a hint.

Your dog, your way

Name the hero after your own dog. Four coats and a wardrobe of scarves, some earned from the stamp book and some sold by Ponta the tanuki.

Potions? No. Onigiri. And it's kind to players: it saves as you go, fights in progress included. If a fight knocks you out twice, it gets a little easier.

Pack your bag at Ponta's

Win fights, earn treats, and spend them at Ponta's travelling shop whenever you stop to rest: snacks to heal, things to throw, charms that change how you fight, and new scarves.

  • Onigiri

    3 treats

    Heals about a third of your HP. Eat it any time, or Snack in a fight.

  • Spirit dango

    5 treats

    Fills your Moon Howl spirit by half.

  • Bento box

    7 treats

    A proper lunch: heals half your HP.

  • Firecracker

    4 treats

    Hits your target and splashes the others; may stun.

  • Smoke bomb
